# 📊 Power BI Advanced Data Analytics Series

Welcome to the Power BI section of my portfolio! Here, I focus on data transformation (ETL) using Power Query, structural modeling using Star Schema, and advanced calculations using DAX.

## 👥 1. HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Dashboard
> **Tools:** Power Query, DAX Measures, KPI Cards, Gender-Age Profiling

### ✨ Project Vision
Is project ka main objective company ke employees ka attrition (turnover) analyze karna tha. Dashboard HR managers ko ye batane mein madad karta hai ki kis department, age group, ya salary slab ke log company chhod kar zyada ja rahe hain.

### 🔍 Key Insights:
* **📉 Attrition Rate:** Overall attrition rate **19.0%** par hai, jisme total 12 employees ne target period mein leave kiya.
* **🎂 Age Group Focus:** Sabse zyada attrition **26-35 age group** mein dekha gaya hai, jo dikhata hai ki mid-level talent ko retain karna zaroori hai.
* **🎓 Education Segment:** Attrition ka ek bada hissa Medical aur Life Sciences background wale employees ka hai.
* **💰 Salary Impact:** Low salary slabs (Upto 5k) mein turnover rate high hai, jo compensation review ki taraf ishara karta hai.

## 🛒 2. Madhav E-Commerce Sales Dashboard
> **Tools:** Matrix Visuals, Monthly Trend Charts, Regional Mapping

### ✨ Project Vision
Ye ek customer-centric e-commerce dashboard hai jo multi-channel retail sales tracking par focus karta hai. Iska main object profit maximization aur channel optimization check karna hai.

### 🔍 Key Insights:
* **💵 Sales Snapshot:** Total sales amount **2,929** generate hui hai jisme cash flow ka ek bada hissa track kiya gaya.
* **💳 Payment Preference:** **COD (Cash on Delivery)** sabse popular payment method hai (47.6%), iske baad EMI aur UPI aate hain.
* **📦 Category Split:** Quantity ke mamle mein **Clothing (42.9%)** market leader hai, jabki Electronics aur Furniture 28.6% par hain.
* **👤 Top Buyer:** Customer analysis ke mutabik *Hemant*, *Shreya*, aur *Chayanika* top buyers hain.

## 🏪 3. Superstore Global Sales Report
> **Tools:** Star Schema Modeling, Donut Charts, Time-Series Forecasting

### ✨ Project Vision
Global Superstore dataset ka end-to-end performance tracking system. Isme multi-year transactions ko handle kiya gaya hai taaki long-term corporate trends pakde ja sakein.

### 🔍 Key Insights:
* **💰 Performance Record:** Dashboard total sales metric **522.44K** showcase karta hai jo business ki massive scalability ko dikhata hai.
* **🛒 Category Dominance:** **Office Supplies** aur **Technology** ne physical products mein Furniture ke muqable behtar perform kiya hai.
* **🚛 Delivery Metrics:** **Standard Class** shipping channel ko log sabse zyada choose karte hain (96K sales volume).
* **👥 Segment Leadership:** Overall sales mein **Consumer segment (44%)** sabse aage hai, iske baad Corporate segment (35%) ka number aata hai.
